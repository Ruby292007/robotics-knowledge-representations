📊 Representation Comparison

Scripts: Excellent for sequential repetitive steps, but fails to capture object-specific handling details.

Frames: Encapsulates rich object properties (Slots & Values), supporting dynamic reasoning, safety checks, and inheritance.

🌐 Real-World Applications
Widely utilized in Service Robotics, Smart Warehouses, Healthcare Assistants, and Smart Home Automation systems where nuanced physical interaction is required.
